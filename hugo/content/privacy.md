_Last updated: July 12, 2026._

This Privacy Policy describes how David Bieber, operating as a sole proprietor,
handles information in connection with a personal AI assistant ("Fable") and its
text-messaging (SMS) service. This policy is publicly accessible and is not
behind any login.

## Who this covers

The SMS service is a private, personal tool. The only recipient of
assistant-initiated text messages is the account owner, David Bieber, himself.
It is not a marketing program and does not send messages to the general public.

## Information collected

In connection with the SMS service, the following may be processed: the account
owner's mobile phone number, and the content of text messages exchanged with the
assistant (for example, reminders, notifications, questions, and replies).

## How information is used

The mobile number and message content are used solely to operate the personal
assistant — to deliver reminders and notifications, to understand and respond to
the account owner's requests, and to maintain the service.

## No sharing with third parties

**We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your mobile phone number, your SMS or
text-messaging consent, or your SMS opt-in information with any third parties or
affiliates for any purpose, including marketing or promotional purposes.**

Messages are delivered through a messaging infrastructure provider (our SMS
carrier) that processes message data solely on our behalf to transmit the
messages; this is not a sale or a sharing of your information with a third party.

## Messaging disclosures

Message frequency varies. **Message and data rates may apply.** Reply **STOP** at
any time to opt out of messages; reply **START** to opt back in; reply **HELP**
for help.
