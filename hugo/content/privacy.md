_Last updated: July 12, 2026._

This Privacy Policy describes how David Bieber, operating as a sole proprietor,
handles information in connection with a personal AI assistant ("Fable") and its
text-messaging (SMS) service.

## Who this covers

The SMS service is a private, personal tool. The only recipient of
assistant-initiated text messages is the account owner, David Bieber, himself.
It is not a marketing program and does not send messages to the general public.

## Information collected

In connection with the SMS service, the following may be processed:

- The account owner's mobile phone number.
- The content of text messages exchanged with the assistant (for example,
  reminders, notifications, questions, and replies).

## How information is used

Message content and the mobile number are used solely to operate the personal
assistant — to deliver reminders and notifications, to understand and respond to
the account owner's requests, and to maintain the service. Messages may be
processed by service providers strictly to operate the assistant, including
Twilio (for message delivery) and AI model providers (to generate responses).

## Information sharing

No mobile information is sold. **No mobile information will be shared with third
parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text-messaging
opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information is
disclosed only to the service providers described above for the sole purpose of
operating the assistant, or where required by law.

## Opt-out and help

Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out of messages; reply **START** to opt back
in; reply **HELP** for help. Message and data rates may apply.
